MarketTripItemController

MarketTripItemController class

Orchestrates adding a scanned (or produce-searched) product to a market trip as purchased, applying an optional price and expiry in one unit of work.

Kept free of UI concerns: navigation, snackbars, and the scan-resolution lifecycle live in the screens. Writes go through shoppingListServiceProvider and the shopping list providers for the trip inventory are invalidated after every mutation.
